# Halloween Projection v0.1.55

Use a PC and projector to bring skulls and bats to walls or floors. Change motion, color and text size with your keyboard, and add your own words.

## Get started

1. Download `halloween-projection-web-v0.1.55-jaw-edge-preview.zip` from Assets, then right-click it and choose **Extract All**.
2. Open `はじめに_START-HERE.html` in Chrome, choose **English**, and select **Start the projection**.
3. Open the projection window, move it to the projector display, and start it there. Return to the console, prepare your text, and select **Enable keys**.

Tested on Windows with Chrome. No extra software or command-line setup is needed. Keep the extracted folder together to use it offline. Set up the projector connection and display arrangement in Windows first.

Use `1`–`8` for motion, `Q W E R T Y U I` for color, and `A S D F G H J K` for text size. `Z` switches between skulls and bats, and `Space` pauses or resumes the visuals. After editing text or switching to another window, return to the console and select **Enable keys** again. See the included guide for all controls.

To blank the image, open **Output, stop & exit** and choose **Stop & release**; choose **Resume** to return. **Esc** stops keyboard controls only.

Start with low brightness and keep the projection beam away from people's eyes. Consider people who are sensitive to bright, changing imagery, and check your equipment and venue before use.
